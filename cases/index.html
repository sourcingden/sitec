Case studies

Sourcing infrastructure, shown not told

Three pieces of work, each as problem → what I did → result. The dashboards are interactive but run on invented numbers: real hiring data stays where it belongs.

Case 01 · Metrics

A funnel you can defend

Problem
Screens were counted differently by different people, so conversion numbers couldn't be compared — or trusted.
What I did
Rebuilt how screens and submissions are counted, then put per-sourcer conversion on live dashboards built on ATS data.
Result
One funnel everyone reads the same way, and real-time pipeline visibility for leadership — sourcing decisions you can defend.

Case 02 · Incentives

Bonuses tied to output

Problem
Bonuses that reward activity push sourcers toward volume, not toward candidates who move forward.
What I did
Designed a performance-based model weighted by screens, submissions and hires, with conversion tiers — and formalised it into policy.
Result
Sourcer incentives tied directly to pipeline outcomes, written down as policy.

    Case 03 · Systems

    From Recruitee to Ashby

    Problem
    A legacy ATS, years of candidate history, and a TA team that had to keep hiring during the switch.
    What I did
    Led the migration end to end — data mapping, transfer, documentation and training.
    Result
    Candidate database and notes carried over, a team wiki for Ashby best practices, and a TA team onboarded through six workshops.
    1. 01

      Map

      Mapped the candidate database, fields and pipeline stages from Recruitee to Ashby.

    2. 02

      Move

      Transferred candidates with their notes and history intact.

    3. 03

      Document

      Built a wiki of Ashby best practices together with the Talent Acquisition Partners.

    4. 04

      Teach

      Ran six interactive workshops to onboard the whole TA team onto the new system.

    5. 05

      Automate

      Automated recurring reporting on top of the new ATS.
