diskevich — Kyiv, Ukraine
crafting idiosyncratic rhythmic journeys that challenge the mind while compelling the body to move

SHORT BIO
diskevich is a Kyiv-based selector and record collector known for unearthing obscure dancefloor heaters. He has run a YouTube channel of music digs since 2017 and has recorded guest mixes for Truancy, Nugs On Board, Breathe Records, X-Kalay, tresydos and LOWMONEYMUSICLOVE.

LONG BIO
Raised and based in Kyiv, diskevich (Denys Dinkevych) spent his formative years on the dancefloor of Closer, the city's most celebrated club, where 90s US house, Detroit techno and minimal house got him hooked. In 2017 he started a YouTube channel of music digs, old and new, that became a go-to for fans of rare house music. His guest mixes for Truancy, Nugs On Board, Breathe Records, X-Kalay, tresydos, LOWMONEYMUSICLOVE and Kalahari Oyster Cult have been played more than 50,000 times on SoundCloud. Truants called him "one of the hardest-working DJs in music right now" when it comes to unearthing obscure dancefloor heaters.

SOUND
Golden-era tech house, chuggy house and obscure dancefloor digs, shaped by 90s US house, Detroit techno and minimal house.
