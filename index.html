Methods

How I search

Method 01

Search angles in 60 seconds

Three one-line directions before writing a single query — each naming what makes it different: stack, geography or title. Pick one in seconds, expand only that.

Method 02

Non-obvious hypotheses

Title swaps, industry pivots, geographic arbitrage, stack-based inference and career-transition patterns — each with its own titles, keywords and donor companies.

Method 03

Market partition

Split the addressable pool along independent axes so segments barely overlap and together cover everything. Stop a segment when it stops producing net-new profiles.

Method 04

Outreach that gets replies

Personalised from the profile, hooked on the role itself, and a little self-aware about being yet another recruiter message. Reply rate is where outreach is won or lost.

Method 05

Metrics you can defend

Count the right thing first: screens, submissions and conversion per sourcer, with bonuses tied to real output. You can't improve what you're measuring wrong.

Method 06

Job posts without fluff

Lead with the real challenge, be straight about what's hard, cut the buzzwords. A good post filters as much as it attracts.
